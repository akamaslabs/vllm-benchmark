1. Executive summary

  1. Data parallelism over 3 GPUs (TP1/DP3) is the winner, by a wide margin and consistently. The optimizer reached 2 186 tok/s per GPU (exp 37); four more DP3 configurations are within 1.5% of it and a sixth within 6%; the best of every other layout stays below 1 420 tok/s per GPU. In absolute terms the tuned DP3 also delivers the most goodput of the whole study (6 559 tok/s), 16% more than the single untuned TP1/DP4 point and almost double the best TP4 (3 312 tok/s) — while leaving one L4 idle.
  2. The best configurations are capped by the load generator, not by the server. Ten DP3 experiments keep both SLAs at the ramp's last level (1 024 concurrent requests; ITL p95 284–291 ms, TTFT p95 0.7–1.3 s). Their top five scores (2 154–2 186) differ by 1.5% with one trial each: exp 37 is the best observed point, not a distinguishable optimum, and the true SLA-compliant capacity of these configurations is unknown because the ramp stopped.
  3. fp8 KV cache is the lever that made the difference inside DP3 (+20%: best auto 1 814 vs best fp8 2 186). It doubles the cache to ~490 k tokens on 3 GPUs, and preemptions track that capacity: all ten runs with ≥ 344 k tokens of KV (≥ 5.3 GiB per GPU, fp8 only) show zero preemptions for the whole trial, the 335–343 k band is mixed, and every run at ≤ 285 k tokens — all the kv-auto ones and the fp8 ones the constraint pushed to gmu ≤ 0.84 — peaks at 10–23 preemptions/s once the ramp fills the cache (except the two 245-sequence runs, whose batch caps demand). fp8 and fp8_e4m3 behave identically, as vLLM documents.
  4. The 2-GPU layouts lose on KV-cache capacity, not on the interconnect — the README's open question. TP2 leaves 1.7–2.2 GiB of KV per GPU (37–49 k tokens at kv auto), the SLA is already breached beyond 96–192 concurrent requests, and PCIe traffic stays at 1.5–2.8 GB/s per GPU. fp8 KV gives TP2 +32% (716 → 942 tok/s per GPU) but it still trails DP3 by 2.3×. TP1/DP2 does not start at all: after weights and activations vLLM finds 0.13 GiB left for KV in exp 6 and none in exp 10.
  5. Pipeline parallelism is the worst family on this node (PP2 571, PP3 557, PP4 445, TP2/PP2 578 tok/s per GPU): high SM activity (0.55–0.74) but pipeline bubbles cap throughput, and the SLA breaks at 96–192 concurrent requests. Cheap collectives do not compensate for the loss of batch-level parallelism.
  6. The recalibrated sampler-warmup constraint worked: zero sampler OOMs in 40 experiments, with max_num_seqs up to 980 at gpu_memory_utilization 0.80, and TP2/DP2 at 256 sequences (exp 5, 754 tok/s per GPU) now starts where study 15's 768 died.
  7. Three failures, two classes: exp 6 and 10 (both TP1/DP2) are a vLLM KV-fit ValueError at start-up, admitted by memory-fit constraint 5; exp 17 ran its full workflow but Akamas' telemetry service did not answer when the trial ended (platform incident, valid configuration lost).

3. Parameter effects and side effects

Attribution caveat. The marginal per-parameter statistics from the analyzer script (e.g. scheduling_policy priority 1 723 vs fcfs 955 mean score, async_scheduling true 1 794 vs false 957) are dominated by which topology the experiment ran on: the optimizer converged into one DP3 region (seqs 490–500, batched tokens 560–700, gmu ≈ 0.877, priority, fp8 KV, async on) and everything outside it is a preset at vLLM defaults. Read the effects below as within-layout contrasts, mostly single trials each.

  • DP3: best auto 1 814 (exp 25) → best fp8 2 186 (exp 37), +20%. With ~9.7 GiB of weights per GPU, auto leaves 4–7.5 GiB of KV (130–245 k tokens for 3 engines); fp8 halves the per-token cost to 48 KiB and reaches ≈ 490 k tokens at the same memory. That is what lets 1 016 concurrent requests stay within ITL 300 ms: every auto experiment tops out at 271–768 concurrency, and 8 of the 10 auto runs peak at 12–23 preemptions/s during the ramp (the exceptions are exp 21 and 24, 245 sequences on 6.7 GiB of KV, peaks 0.8–3.6/s). Preemptions vanish above ≈ 344 k tokens of KV (≥ 5.3 GiB per GPU, reachable only with fp8): the ten runs above that line (exp 26, 28, 30, 31, 34, 36–38, 40, 42) show a flat zero; the 335–343 k band is mixed (exp 35 zero, exp 43 at 10/s); the fp8 runs the constraint pushed down to gmu 0.80–0.84 with 630–980 sequences (3.5–4.4 GiB, 232–285 k tokens: exp 27, 33, 39, 41) preempt like the auto ones and score 1 800–1 970.
  • TP2: auto 716 (exp 7) → fp8 942 (exp 9), +32%; KV grows from 2.2 to 2.1 GiB… of fp8 tokens, i.e. 49 k → 92 k tokens. Still KV-starved: SLA-max concurrency 136 → 192.
  • TP4: fp8 hurts (exp 8, 580 vs exp 1, 607, both ~190 concurrent): with 9.2 GiB of KV and 12% cache usage TP4 is not KV-limited, so the quantisation only adds cost. Rule: fp8 KV pays off exactly when the layout is KV-bound.
  • fp8 vs fp8_e4m3: near-twins exp 34 (fp8, 2 054) and exp 31 (fp8_e4m3, 2 052) confirm they are the same kernel on CUDA (vLLM maps fp8 to e4m3). Pooling them is legitimate.

  • TP2 on 2 GPUs: weights 14.5 GiB + ~2.3 GiB of overhead + the activation peak of 8 192 batched tokens leave 1.7–2.2 GiB for KV. PCIe traffic is the lowest of all multi-GPU layouts (1.5–2.8 GB/s per GPU), so the interconnect is not what limits it.
  • TP1/DP2 (exp 6, 10) does not start: the DP replica has the same 14.5 GiB of sharded experts but a full-width activation peak, and vLLM computes 0.13 GiB available for KV ("estimated maximum model length 1456"). Constraint 5 (gmu × 22.5 − 29.03/(TP·DP·PP) ≥ 4) assumed 4 GiB of headroom for this layout; the real headroom is ≈ 0. Both attempts (kv auto/EP on, kv fp8/EP off) failed identically, so neither KV dtype nor EP rescues it at max_num_batched_tokens 8192.
  • TP4: 4–6.5 GB/s of PCIe per GPU (two all-reduces per layer over PCIe), 9–10.7 GiB of KV mostly unused (6–37%). Even tuned (exp 2, 768 seqs) it reaches 828 per GPU / 3 312 total: the collectives, not memory, bound it — exactly the layout NCCL tuning (ROADMAP §E) should target if TP4 must be used.
  • Pipeline parallelism (PP2/3/4, TP2/PP2): plenty of KV (2.6–10 GiB per GPU), the lowest PCIe traffic of the study (0–3 GB/s), SM activity 0.55–0.74 on every stage — and the worst goodput. Per-GPU throughput falls with the number of stages (571 → 557 → 445): the stages are busy but serialised (pipeline bubbles at the small per-stage micro-batches a 256-sequence budget allows). The TP2-vs-PP2 head-to-head on 2 GPUs goes to TP2 (716 vs 571 at identical settings, +25%); TP4-vs-PP4 goes to TP4 (607–828 vs 445).
  • TP1/DP4 (exp 3): the only 4-GPU layout with per-GPU goodput comparable to DP3 (1 416 at 768 seqs, kv auto, untuned) and the highest total of the presets/imports (5 662). It never got a tuned or fp8 trial — see recommendations.

4. Parameter interactions

The analyzer's pairwise scan flags two interactions, both artefacts of the sampling: kv_cache_dtype × async_scheduling (best kv is fp8_e4m3 when async is off, fp8 when on) and kv_cache_dtype × scheduling_policy — the two fp8 spellings are the same kernel, and the split only mirrors which presets used which spelling. The one real interaction in the data is KV dtype × topology: fp8 KV helps the KV-bound layouts (TP2 +32%, DP3 +20%) and hurts the KV-rich one (TP4 −4%), see §3.1. Cell coverage for topology × kv × EP outside DP3 is one experiment per cell, so no further interaction can be supported.

Constraint behaviour

  • Sampler-warmup guard (constraint 2, recalibrated): no sampler OOM in any of the 40 started experiments — including max_num_seqs 980 (exp 39), 912 (exp 33), 768 on the imported points, and TP2/DP2 at 256 (exp 5) which had died at 768 in study 15. The fix study 16 exists for is confirmed. The analyzer flags experiments 2, 3, 4 as violating this constraint: they are the points imported from study 15 with max_num_seqs 768, deliberately kept as valid measurements outside the new feasible region (README).
  • Memory-fit constraint 5 is wrong for data-parallel replicas: it models the per-GPU footprint as 29.03 / (TP·DP·PP) + 4 GiB, but a DP rank carries a full-width activation peak (sized by max_num_batched_tokens 8 192 and max_model_len) that a TP rank splits. TP2 fits with ~2 GiB of KV; DP2 does not fit at all. Both DP2 presets consumed a 10-minute rollout each.
  • Constraint 6 (EP required at 4 ranks) was never tested: no TP×DP = 4 layout ran with EP off (the constraint forbade it) — the smoke_test.sh tp4-noep check the README calls for is still pending.
  • Constraint 7 (no async with PP) held; no PP experiment was proposed by the optimizer, so it was exercised only by the presets.
  • Goal constraints (TTFT ≤ 1 500, ITL ≤ 300): all 40 scored experiments have a compliant window (goal status VALID for all). The analyzer's metricConstraintsViolations margins are negative for every experiment, i.e. they describe some other window of the trial, and were not used.
  • Optimizer coverage: with numberOfInitExperiments: 0 and 12 presets as the only design, the optimizer proposed TP1/DP3 in 26 of 27 asks. It never revisited TP1/DP4 (one point, 1 416 at 768 seqs) or TP2/DP2 (one point), and the single TP2 ask (exp 32, 129 seqs) scored 651.

7. Caveats and data quality

  • Stopped early, on purpose. The team finished the study manually on 2026-09-20 to contain the g6.12xlarge cost (≈ 1 h 12 min per experiment); 27 of 100 optimizer experiments ran. The optimizer had converged on one region and was sampling its neighbourhood, so more experiments would have refined the DP3 plateau rather than explored other layouts.
  • Single trial per experiment (numberOfTrials: 1). The top five scores span 1.5%; treat them as one plateau.
  • Ramp ceiling. Ten experiments are scored at the last level; their capacity is a lower bound and the ranking among them is not informative.
  • Export bundle was incomplete (Akamas 3.7.x): no last-optimization.json, no logs.json, and only 22 of ~290 metric files — deterministically, on three exports, missing the goal's own metrics. The per-experiment table was rebuilt from optimizations.json (last snapshot + the final ask for exp 43) and cross-checked against akamas list experiment (all 43 scores match). All metric series were re-fetched from the cluster's Prometheus (10-day retention) with the telemetry instance's own PromQL at 30 s; the SLA-window reconstruction reproduces Akamas' score for all 40 scored experiments to 0.00%, which validates the mapping, the data and the window semantics (6 contiguous samples, window-mean TTFT ≤ 1 500 and ITL ≤ 300, maximal prefill throughput).
  • Experiment numbering. The optimizer arrays exclude the imported baseline (index i = experiment i + 2); this report uses Akamas' 1-based experiment numbers throughout, matching the UI.
  • SLA-max concurrency is the ramp level nearest to running + waiting requests in the scored window (waiting is ≈ 0 in every scored window, so it equals the admitted batch); it is inferred, not read from AIPerf.
  • Non-KV footprint is derived (framebuffer used − KV capacity × bytes/token/GPU); it is consistent with the README's measured 7.4 GiB of weights at TP4 and 2.3 GiB of CUDA/NCCL overhead, but it is an estimate.
  • Logs. Akamas' Elasticsearch log service rejected large INFO queries ("all shards failed"); root causes were recovered with small pages. The failed experiments' vLLM stderr is quoted from those pages.
  • Imported points (exp 1–4) carry study 15's max_num_seqs 768 and vLLM defaults for non-rendered parameters (the table shows pack defaults for exp 1's un-rendered knobs, not verified effective values).

8. Recommendations for the follow-up

  1. Tune TP1/DP4 with fp8 KV, and TP2 on 2 GPUs with fp8 KV, in the region the optimizer found (seqs ≈ 500, gmu ≈ 0.877, priority, async on). DP4 has one untuned point at 1 416 per GPU / 5 662 total; if it follows DP3's +55% from tuning it would beat 6 559 total. Either force the topology with presets or run separate short studies per layout — the optimizer will not leave DP3 on its own.
  2. Extend or re-floor the ramp (e.g. 12 levels 96 → 4 096, or add levels 1 448 / 2 048) before comparing the top DP3 configurations: at 1 024 they are not distinguished.
  3. Fix constraint 5 for DP replicas: require tensor_parallel_size ≥ 2 || data_parallel_size ≥ 3 || pipeline_parallel_size ≥ 2 when the product is 2 — or model the activation peak explicitly (a DP rank at max_num_batched_tokens 8 192 needs ≈ 4–5 GiB). Both TP1/DP2 presets were wasted rollouts.
  4. Pin kv_cache_dtype to fp8 for KV-bound layouts (DP3, DP4, TP2) and drop fp8_e4m3 from the domain (same kernel; halves a categorical's cells). Keep auto only if TP4 stays in scope.
  5. Drop pipeline parallelism from the next topology study on this node (worst family at every GPU count), unless the goal changes to a latency-bound one where PP's low TTFT at low load matters.
  6. Add queue time to the SLA or to the KPIs' interpretation: the top windows queue requests for 5–9 s (p95) while meeting TTFT/ITL. If end-to-end latency matters, add e2e_request_latency_p95 as a goal constraint.
  7. Phase 2 (NCCL tuning, ROADMAP §E) should target TP4 and DP3/DP4, the layouts where PCIe traffic is 3–6.5 GB/s per GPU; TP2 and PP are not interconnect-bound.
