  experiment 2 (+50 %) came from the per-instance max_num_seqs 256 cap of the baseline values,

  configurations in the same band tie. A follow-up with finer levels between 512 and 640 is needed

  then Akamas marked the experiment ERROR: ~40 min lost.

  (tested locally), so the truncation is on the Akamas side.
- Fix (apply_config.sh): log the raw params.env line; a watermark above the study domain maximum
  0.10 can only be a tiny value without its exponent, so it is replaced with 0 and a WARNING is
  logged. Copied to the toolbox after experiment 11's apply step finished.
