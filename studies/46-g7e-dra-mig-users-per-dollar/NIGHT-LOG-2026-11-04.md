Observations:

- The score follows max_num_seqs. Every run with a low cap (≤ 268 per replica, or 142 × 2) lands
  at ~255 whatever the GPU split; the two runs with a high cap (520, 772) land at ~510.
- With tuned parameters, whole GPU and 2 MIG slices tie (508.7 vs 507.7). The MIG gain of
  experiment 2 (+50 %) came from the per-instance max_num_seqs 256 cap of the baseline values,
  not from splitting the GPU.
- The ramp is coarse above 512 (next level 640 for the whole GPU, 2 × 320 for two slices), so
  configurations in the same band tie. A follow-up with finer levels between 512 and 640 is needed
  to separate the best ones.
